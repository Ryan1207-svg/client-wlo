001 Chat Channels: Local Chat, Whisper, Team Chat,
    Guild Chat and World Chat.
    Local Chat applies to players in the same area.
    Whisper allows you to speak privately with a
    target player.
    Team Chat applies to other players in your team.
    Guild Chat applies to players in the same 
    organization.
    World Chat can be read by all players in the server.

016 Players must defeat enemies completely to gain more
    experience and obtain the items carried by enemies.
    When players are in a group, the last one to defeat
    the enemy obtains the items carried by the enemy.
    Depending on the speed of the players involved in 
    the fight, 2 or more players may end up killing a
    monster together.
    In this situation, the experience and the item 
    gained will be multiplied by the number of
    fighting players, and then be evenly distributed 
    among them.
027 The forces of Earth, Water, Fire and Wind are 
    subdued by each other.
    Water is held together by Earth, Fire is 
    extinguished by Water,
    Wind is manipulated by Fire, and Earth is slowly 
    erased by Wind.
    It is vitally important to understand their 
    relationships for players to win the battle.
    After leveling, the character's ability points can 
    be adjusted at the lower right-hand corner of the 
    main interface.
    Ability points include STR, CON, WIT,WIS and AGI.
    Increasing STR affects ATK, increasing CON upgrades HP,
    increasing INT influences the effects of MATK;
    while increasing WIS affects the SP value and MDEF;
    and increasing AGI enhances SPD.
043 The attributes players select at the beginning of 
    the game will affect the skills players will learn 
    later.
    Some skills are learned automatically after players 
    reach a certain level, while some skills are learned 
    after they have reached the top levels.
    Some skills are learned as a special reward for 
    completing quests given to players.
    Skills can also be upgraded after being used many 
    times by players, representing proficiency in the 
    skill. The higher the level the skill is, the more 
    powerful its effects will be.
    Skills are also related with weapons the players 
    hold.
    For example, if your character is holding magic
    sticks, these would correspondingly affect your 
    magic skills.
    It is very useful to understand the relationship 
    between skills and weapons for players to win 
    battles.
    
064 When players are involved in fighting,
    a circle menu appears around them which have options
    of attack, skill, defense, capture, escape, items 
    and AUTO.
    Attack - Attacking with non magical common weapon.
    Skill - Lists available skills for use.
    Defense - A defensive state with no attack.
    Capture - Allows players to capture enemies as
    their pets.
    Escape - Allows players to quit a fight.
    Items - Allows players to choose an item for use.
    AUTO - Allows players to attack an enemy with the 
    same skill they used in the last round.
    If no skill was used, a common attack will be used.
    Capture is used to capture enemies as pets and to 
    listen to players' orders.
    When capturing an enemy, the lower the target 
    enemies HP is, the higher the rate of success.
    If players click on an enemy directly, the attack 
    will be common attack.

142 Basic Game Knowledge                               
    01.What's the difference between these main
       characters?
       Each of them has different original points and
       inherent skills.                             
    02.How to recover HP and SP?
       In addition to having something to eat to 
       recover HP and SP, pressing ALT +1 to sit down
       and having a good rest can also recover HP and
       SP. If there is a bathtub, it can be used to
       speed up recovery.                                    
    03.How to collect?   
       Left click on items to collect.Mushrooms,flowers,
       wood and shale can also be collected by left 
       clicking on them. After breaking boxes, treasure 
       cases, casks and vases, you will find useful
       items inside.                                       
    04.How to use a vacuum?
       Click on collecting points after the vacuum has
       been equipped.             
    05.How to use fishing rod?
       Open inventory when you are close to the water.
       Double click to begin fishing.                  
    06.How to get a tent?
       Talk to the Astrologian in Kelan Village.                                     
    07.How to use a tent?
       Open inventory and click tent to open it.
       You can close the tent by right 
       clicking it. Tent can be used as a commercial
       real estate to do business.
       If other players' tents block your way, you can
       right click to move forward, or choose not
       to display the players' tents from the character
       information display in system options.                                       
    08.How to select many items and pack them up at the
       first attempt?
       Press Ctrl,click items to select whatever you
       want and pack them up at the first attempt.        
    09.How to use alchemy?
       Compounding is alchemy. The result of compounding
       is related to the level of alchemy.                                           
    10.How to use world chat?
       A wireless radio set will be given upon
       completing the Dinner Incident quest.Then World
       chat can be used by spending 15 sp.                        
    11.How to run a vending stall?
       Click on Business on the interface to run a
       vending stall. Movement is not allowed while
       running a vending stall.                         
    12.What if pet inventory is full?
       Hotels in Holy village and Welling Village are
       used to shelter pets.                                               
    13.How to visit other players' stall?
       Double click the stall names.                                  
    14.How to get a coconut?
       Click the coconut tree to get a coconut.        
    15.How to meet enemies?
       There are two different kinds of enemies.One has
       been indicated on map.
       You will enter a fight automatically upon meeting
       them. The other will 
       be met randomly while you are walking.                                       
    16.How to return to the city?
       You can return to city by the reborn point in
       system option.        
    17.What is the punishment after characters die?
       If characters die, you will lose your experience
       and have to wait for rebirth.                         
    18.How to set nickname?
       There is a nickname setting in Social or press
       Ctrl+E to set a nickname.                         
    19.How to get tent enlarged?
       Talk to the Official in Holy Village to get your
       tent enlarged by giving him a Title Deed and
       5 stars.                 
    20.How to use Resource Recycling 
       Put items into Resource Recycling to make new
       items.                                             
    21.What if the warehouse does not have enough space?
       Build a storeroom to store items or make use of
       Coin-Op Games to store.                          
    22.How to cancel selected items while compounding?
       Right click on items you want to cancel.        
    23.How to use computer?
       After it starts to make a prop, click pause,
       repeat this operation five times and click on
       the computer to check out scheduling.                                               
    24.What to do if the gun is broken?
       Well, it can't be repaired.                                      
    25.How to use Stone Knife and planner tool?
       Drag them on the work platform,and then click the
       platform.                                              
    26.How to PK?
       You can find NPCs and all players who didn't turn
       off the PK function to PK by clicking them and
       "join combat".                                   
    27.How to cancel the action for next step during a
       battle?
       Right click.                           
    28.How to fight automatically?
       There is a function for you to fight 
       automatically.If you cast spells, the function
       will allow you to continue to cast spells until
       you don't have enough HP or SP.Then ordinary
       attack.                                         
    29.What i am gonna do if i forgot my quest content?
       Go find the Astrologian in Kelan Village,she will
       give a notepad from which you can know all the
       information about your quests.                    
    30.Why are my pets lost?
       If your pets' Friendship Brooch is less than 20,
       they will run away and the pets are able to
       join some important battle, if their Friendship 
       Brooch is more than 40. so it's important to
       increase the Friendship Brooch of your pets.             
    31.How to increase pets' Friendship?
       By doing some special quests or improving your pets' 
       levels,you can increase the pets' Friendship.   
    32.There is a heart shape at the bottom right corner
       of the interface, what does that stand for?
       It is the "Make Friends" function, the heart
       shape will appear when someone who is perfect for
       you is approaching (Perfection is estimated by
       using objective personal information).                      

    33.How to obtain cheap replenishments?
       You can obtain some raw material by fishing,and
       then use a pan or some other tools to cook them
       to obtain cheap replenishment.                                 
    34.How to ride on pets?
       Click the opinion "group", and choose "ride".
